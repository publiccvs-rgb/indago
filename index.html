The industry

What is virtual life insurance sales?

Licensed agents help families protect their income, mortgage and final expenses. Everything happens over the phone or video call, so you can work from home or anywhere else.

There's no storefront and no door knocking. Clients ask to be contacted, you talk them through their options, and you're paid a commission on every policy you write. Your income comes from how much you produce, not from hours or seniority.

Getting licensed is straightforward and usually takes a few weeks. Most new agents struggle with what comes after: finding people to call, getting through enough calls, and keeping the clients they've written. That's what our systems handle.
