    The industry

    What is virtual life insurance sales?

    Licensed agents help families protect their income, mortgage and final expenses, entirely by phone and video. You're paid commission on every policy you write, so your income follows your production.

    45% of Americans have no life insurance at all
    70% of households would struggle if the primary wage earner passed
    $10K is the cost of an average funeral today
    The work
    Call people who asked about coverage, find what fits their budget, and get it in place.
    The pay
    Commission on every policy, uncapped. No salary ceiling and no seniority ladder.
    The start
    A state license, usually a few weeks. The hard part is leads and volume, and that's covered.

    Advance
    The share of commission paid upfront.
